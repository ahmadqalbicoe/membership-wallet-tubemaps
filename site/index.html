UK shopping centres tube map

Every shopping centre, mall and outlet in the longlist, drawn as a metro network. Each line is a region of the UK and each station is a local authority; the bigger the station, the more centres it holds. Lines meet at interchanges where regions border each other, and a ferry joins Northern Ireland to Scotland. Click a station to see its centres, owners, managing agents and pension fund status, or plan a route across the country.
