Shopping Malls Tube Map

Every UK shopping centre, mall and outlet in the longlist, drawn as a metro network, with the brands inside them, the student benefit schemes around them and the retailers' affiliate routes. Each line is a region of the UK and each station is a local authority; the bigger the station, the more centres it holds. Lines meet at interchanges where regions border each other, and a ferry joins Northern Ireland to Scotland. Click a station to see its centres, owners, managing agents and pension fund status, or plan a route across the country.

Notes, assumptions and caveats

Built from UK Shopping Centres by Local Authority (FINAL) and the Batch 1 research update (November 2026): 504 centres across 261 local authorities, about 70% of the c.700 UK centres. Sources include Savills, Knight Frank, NBIM, CPP Investments, UK Malls Directory, company press releases and Wikipedia's list of UK shopping centres. Station positions are schematic: they follow geography loosely, with London and the big cities stretched out so they can be read.
